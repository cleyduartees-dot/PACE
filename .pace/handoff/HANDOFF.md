## Mission

```
MISSION_VERSION 0.1.0

STATUS APPROVED

OWNER President Cley Duarte

MISSION

PACE exists so that people and AIs can work for years across one or
hundreds of projects without ever losing the knowledge, the context, or
the correct way of working.

PACE does for a project's knowledge what Git did for its source code.

PROBLEM

Today, engineering knowledge lives scattered across ChatGPT
conversations, Claude conversations, Slack, Notion, Word documents,
READMEs, emails, and the architect's own memory. When the AI changes, the
team changes, or months pass, most of that knowledge disappears — and the
same question returns every time: "explain the project to me again."
PACE exists so that never happens again.

END
```

## Vision

```
VISION_VERSION 0.1.0

STATUS APPROVED

OWNER President Cley Duarte

VISION

Five years from now: dozens of independent projects, each with thousands
of decisions made, thousands of conversations, thousands of documents,
thousands of changes. With PACE, any AI can arrive tomorrow and
reconstruct any of them in minutes — not because it "remembers", but
because the project knows how to explain itself.

WHAT_PACE_GOVERNS

All the engineering knowledge of a project: Mission (why it exists),
Vision (where it's going), Roadmap (what's missing), Sprint (what's being
done today), Decisions (why X was decided, why not Y), History (how it
evolved), Releases (what changed), Requests (what the President asked
for, what's still pending), Memory (what an AI must remember), Context
(what someone new needs to know).

EXPECTATIONS

1  Any AI can join a project in minutes, without asking what we're doing,
   what's missing, or why it was built this way.

2  Knowledge never gets lost again. Every significant discovery becomes
   official knowledge, not something that stays trapped in a single chat.

3  Every AI works the same way regardless of which one it is — today or
   in the future — all reconstructing exactly the same context.

4  Projects can last decades, surviving a complete change of the people
   who started them.

5  New projects get created in minutes: pace create produces a
   repository, structure, memory, mission, vision, roadmap, contract and
   governance, all ready to start.

6  PACE learns continuously. Every permanent observation from the
   President can become, after review and approval, a policy, a
   protocol, a standard, or a rule of behavior for AIs — see
   PROMOTED_FROM in ontology/PACE_ONTOLOGY_0.1.0.pdl, which already
   models exactly this relation.

FINAL_OBJECTIVE

Build an engineering operating system capable of governing the
knowledge, evolution and collaboration between people and artificial
intelligences for any project, preserving its memory, its architecture
and its way of working for its entire life.

END
```
